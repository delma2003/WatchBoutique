Wrist Size Guide

How to Measure Your Wrist

  1. Use a flexible measuring tape or a strip of paper
  2. Wrap it around your wrist just below the wrist bone
  3. Mark where the ends meet and measure against a ruler
  4. Alternatively, use a string and measure its length
